The straightforward version

Privacy, without the fine print.

Daymark is a browser-based planner. Here’s what that means for your information.

In short: Daymark does not require an account and does not send your tasks or profile to a Daymark server. Planner information is saved in local storage in the browser you are using.

Information stored on your device

Daymark stores the tasks you create, your optional display name, and the state of the focus timer in your browser’s local storage. This lets the planner remember your information when you return on the same browser. The information is not synced between devices.

Analytics and advertising

The current Daymark app does not include analytics, advertising, session recording, or third-party tracking scripts. Its styles and scripts are served from the same site as the pages.

Your choices

  • Use Settings to download a backup, restore a valid backup, or clear the app’s local data.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove locally stored information.
  • Remember that anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to use this planner or view its locally stored information.

Hosting and changes

If Daymark is hosted publicly, the hosting provider may process ordinary connection data (such as an IP address) to deliver and protect the website. The provider’s own privacy terms apply to that infrastructure. This notice describes the current standalone app and should be updated if analytics, accounts, syncing, or other services are added.

Contact

This demo does not currently provide a support or privacy contact address. A contact method should be added before operating Daymark as a public service.
